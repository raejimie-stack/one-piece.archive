01 — STORY

A small boat.
A huge dream.

Volume 1 introduces Monkey D. Luffy and the dream that will define his journey: finding the legendary One Piece and becoming the Pirate King. Along the way, he encounters Koby, crosses paths with Roronoa Zoro, and begins gathering the people who will become part of his adventure.
